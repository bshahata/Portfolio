02 / HOW I THINK

Make the next action clear.

A generalized workflow analysis framework from my portfolio. Illustrative—not a company SOP, internal deliverable, or deployed dashboard.

  1. 01

    Review demand

    Compare reservations, expected returns, and near-term requirements.

  2. 02

    Assess readiness

    Check availability, maintenance, and preparation constraints.

  3. 03

    Find bottlenecks

  4. 04

    Coordinate

    Prioritize allocations and transfers; communicate schedule changes.

  5. 05

    Monitor & adjust

    Review throughput, delays, and quality as conditions change.

Explore the illustrative KPI framework +

Fleet availability

Ready vehicles compared with required vehicles for the same location and period. Supports capacity and transfer decisions.

Throughput

Vehicles completing a defined preparation or movement stage per period. Helps locate slow stages and handoff constraints.

Delay rate

Delayed activities divided by comparable activities, using an agreed delay definition. Helps investigate recurring causes.

Quality performance

Activities meeting agreed criteria divided by comparable activities reviewed. Checks that faster execution maintains quality.
